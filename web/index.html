CALPHAD in the browser

Pick two elements, and get a phase diagram computed in this tab — no server, nothing uploaded. Pyodide supplies CPython compiled to WebAssembly; POUNCE solves each restricted Gibbs-energy minimization, and discopt-calphad generates the composition sets. Points appear as they are solved. What this page cannot do is certify: the global proof that no phase is missing runs through discopt's Rust solver, which has no WebAssembly build. Every point here is a local equilibrium that has been searched for destabilizing phases — hover the diagram to read the composition under the cursor, and click a point to see how far its certificate actually goes.
